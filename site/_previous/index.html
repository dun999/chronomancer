What is available today? +

The browser demo runs immediately, with simulated outcomes. The Telegram bot supports paper onboarding, market discovery, prophecies, settlement, streaks, and a leaderboard. Running your own bot requires a Telegram bot token. Live testnet execution requires a funded operator wallet and explicit operator access; per-player wallets are future work.

What happens when a market is voided? +

When a market cannot resolve reliably, each outcome contract redeems at half its face value. In Telegram practice, that is half your contract payout in sand. This can differ from your original stake.
