Getting startedfour steps

From a cold start to a mined receipt.

No exchange signup, no seed phrase to write down. The bot makes a wallet for you, says exactly what it is holding, and never signs anything you have not read.

01/start

A wallet appears.

One tap creates a Somnia Shannon wallet keyed to your Telegram account. The server holds the encrypted key and signs only what you confirm.

02/wallet

Fund it with test tokens.

Your address, live STT for gas and tUSDC for settlement, plus both faucets. Funding is checked on chain before any order.

03/market

Find the moment.

Live binary markets with real asks on both outcomes — or just ask. The assistant reads snapshots and source-linked news, and abstains when it has nothing.

04Confirm

Sign, then read the truth.

The review shows side, budget, limit and expiry. After it mines, the receipt is decoded from the chain: actual fills, and a hash you can check yourself.

What it will not doread twice
It abstains. With no source-linked evidence it says so rather than inventing a thesis. Book prices alone do not establish an edge.
Conviction is not probability. Any confidence it expresses is an uncalibrated opinion, not a win rate.
It never assumes a fill. Every number in a receipt is decoded from the mined transaction.
No paper balances. Positions come from on-chain outcome balances and indexed average-cost P/L, excluding gas.
It is custodial. The server holds your encrypted key and signs what you confirm — a real trade-off, made for a testnet prototype.
It is test money. tUSDC and STT are testnet tokens. A settled market is a real transaction, not a real dollar.
